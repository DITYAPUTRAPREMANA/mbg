# 🥗 MBG — Meal Based Nutrition Guide

> **Manage your diet smarter. Scan your food. Know your nutrition.**

MBG is a mobile application that helps users track their daily nutrition by detecting food items directly from the camera and automatically calculating their nutritional values.

---

## 📱 Overview

MBG (Meal Based Nutrition Guide) is a diet management app designed to make healthy eating accessible and effortless. Using real-time image recognition, users can simply point their camera at any meal to instantly receive a breakdown of ingredients along with their macro and micro nutritional profiles.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Instant Food Scanning
- Point your camera at any meal or ingredients
- Real-time food detection with confidence score (e.g., "pepper — 86% Match")
- Supports detection of multiple food items in a single frame

### 🧪 Nutritional Analysis
- Automatic calculation of nutritional values per 100g
- Displays macro and micro nutrient profiles
- Covers a wide range of ingredients including vegetables, proteins, and more

### 📋 Detected Ingredients List
- Lists all detected items with their calorie information
- Each item can be individually removed from the scan result
- Supports up to 12+ items per scan

### 📊 Daily Nutrition Tracking
- Dashboard showing daily calorie intake (e.g., 1,840 / 2,200 kcal)
- Visual progress bars for macronutrients (Protein, Fat, Carbs)
- Recent meal history log

### 🕓 Meal History
- View past scans and logged meals
- Organized history for easy review (e.g., Grilled Chicken Salad, Berry Oatmeal Bowl)

---

## 🗂️ Navigation

The app uses a bottom navigation bar with four main sections:

- **Dashboard** — Nutrition overview and daily summary
- **Scan** — Camera-based food detection (primary feature)
- **History** — Past meal logs and scan results
- **Profile** — User preferences and account settings

## 📌 Notes

- Calorie values shown are **per 100g** of each detected ingredient
- Confidence scores indicate how certain the model is about each detected food item
- Some items may show **0.0 kcal per 100g** if nutritional data is pending or unavailable
